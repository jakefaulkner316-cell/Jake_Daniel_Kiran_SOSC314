HOW TO READ THIS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- If both methods give the same sign and the same significance verdict,
  the regression conclusion does not depend on how ads are combined
  into quarters, even if the magnitudes differ.
- Sign differences are only meaningful when the estimates are clearly
  away from zero; a flip between two near-zero, non-significant
  estimates means 'no detectable effect' under both methods.
- Pooling gives long records more weight. Long records are
  disproportionately financial notices, supplements and advertorials
  (Week 4), so pooled results describe overall advertising language
  and average-of-ratios results describe the typical advertisement.
- All results are descriptive associations, not causal effects.
