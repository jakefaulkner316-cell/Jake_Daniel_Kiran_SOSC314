INTERPRETATION GUIDE
----------------------------------------------------------------------

If the recession coefficient changes substantially after GDP growth is added, part of the original recession association may reflect broader changes in economic growth rather than recession status alone.

If the coefficient remains similar, the recession relationship is more robust to controlling for GDP growth.

The GDP-only model helps determine whether sentiment responds more directly to continuous changes in economic growth rather than to the binary recession classification.
